# InvoiceQuick vs. competitors

**Nearest competitor: Invoice Simple** (also compared: Zoho Invoice, Wave, Invoice2go).

| | InvoiceQuick | Invoice Simple | Invoice2go | Zoho / Wave |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $4.99 once | Subscription (~$5-$15/mo) | Subscription | Free tier, upsells/payments fees |
| Account required | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Works offline, data stays on device | Yes | No (cloud) | No | No |
| Runs in any browser | Yes | App + web | App + web | Web |
| PDF export | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |

## Why InvoiceQuick is better
- **One-time $4.99, no subscription.** Common review complaint about Invoice Simple and Invoice2go: recurring fees and paywalled limits.
- **No sign-up, no ads, private.** Invoices never leave your browser.
- **Fast:** line items, tax, currency, auto-save, PDF in under a minute.
